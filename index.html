How the recommendation works

Decision support, not a replacement for CRM KYP or final human review.

1. Eligibility firstPartner hospital → current status → insurer panel → TPA panel → city/distance.
2. Historical evidenceExact procedure + insurer + TPA is preferred. If too sparse, the tool clearly labels insurer-level or procedure-only fallback evidence.
3. EconomicsTarget bill fit, approval realization and bill-vs-approval deductions are scored separately.
4. Recency & confidenceLast exact-match case, last procedure case, usable sample size and data completeness affect confidence.
5. Current commentsMatching “hold / do not give / low package / restricted” comments are highlighted and excluded by default when clearly relevant.
6. Human choiceThe KYP team gives 4–5 suitable options; the patient chooses the hospital.
