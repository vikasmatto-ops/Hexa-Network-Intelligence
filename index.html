How Ranking Works
1. Empanelment firstSheet 1 must show the hospital as a partner and the selected insurer + TPA as eligible.
2. Target is a benchmarkBelow-target ATS is penalised. Hospitals are not penalised for billing above target; stronger ATS is rewarded with an outlier cap.
3. Business outcomeAverage Bill / ATS and average approval amount drive ranking, while deduction % controls for poor insurer realization.
4. Same-procedure evidenceOrdinary procedures are matched from the Procedure field, then filtered to the exact insurer + TPA. Free-text remarks do not create a false procedure match.
5. RecencyLast exact match and last procedure date prevent stale history from looking current.
6. Operational commentsHold / low package / do-not-give instructions are surfaced before the option is shared.
